# Competitors

Research from October 2026. Figures come from the sources below; some are several years old, as noted.

## Volunteering platforms in France

The closest to Aido today. All are listing sites: each has its own catalogue and sign-up, and applying happens on the association's side.

| Platform | What it is | How Aido differs |
|---|---|---|
| JeVeuxAider.gouv.fr | Government platform. 680,000 registered volunteers and 20,000 missions (5-year report, March 2025) | One of Aido's sources, not a competitor. Aido adds the conversation, the other sources and the web, and applying without a form |
| France Bénévolat | Long-standing network. Volunteers and associations create an account; associations pay a membership fee | Free for associations, no account needed to search |
| Tous Bénévoles | Listings plus a hotline and training for associations | Classic listing site, no AI |
| Benevolt | Started for young retirees, open to all ages since 2020, with remote volunteering (tutoring, mentoring) | Narrower audience, no application written for the volunteer |
| Diffuz | Run by Macif: volunteering as challenges set by partner associations | One-off challenge format. Still active? To check |
| Benenova | Association running short group volunteering sessions (1 to 4 hours), also for company staff | Organizes events itself; Aido matches people to missions |

## Employee volunteering platforms

The market Aido would charge (see "Business model" in the README).

| Platform | What we know |
|---|---|
| Vendredi | 160 corporate clients (TikTok, Danone) and 1,000 associations |
| Alaya | Bought by Benevity in 2021; used by more than 90 companies in Europe, including France |
| Wenabi, microDON, Yumana, Day-one | Named as engagement platforms in a 2020 Carenews article; current status not checked |
| Benevity, Goodera | Large international platforms for corporate giving and volunteering |

## Abroad

VolunteerMatch and Idealist (US listing sites), Catchafire (skills-based volunteering). No funded startup found doing AI volunteer matching, only student and community projects such as Volunty (Japan).

## Positioning

French platforms are listing sites, each with its own catalogue. Corporate platforms serve only employees. Aido searches all sources in one conversation and removes the form. For the jury: JeVeuxAider is a source, not a competitor, and Vendredi shows companies pay.
